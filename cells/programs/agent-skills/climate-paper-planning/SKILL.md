# Climate paper planning

Local planning skill grounded in journal policies; not independently validated.

1. **Check policy first.** Identify the journal and manuscript type. Verify the
   current AI, disclosure, confidentiality, and code/data policies before asking
   for manuscript material or offering substantive help. If the venue is unknown
   or a policy cannot be verified, ask for it and limit help to finding the
   relevant requirements. Apply the target venue's policy even if another skill
   recommends drafting text or interpreting results.
2. **Map evidence where permitted.** Ask the authors for their research questions
   and intended claims. For each claim, record the evidence the authors identify,
   its diagnostic and provenance, and any gap or uncertainty they need to resolve.
   Mark unsupported claims; leave interpretation and manuscript prose to the
   authors where required.
3. **Plan figures and archives.** Where allowed, map figures and tables to their
   evidence and list inputs, scripts, parameters, run manifests, identifiers,
   licenses and disclosure obligations. Check redistribution rights before
   recommending that any input be archived.
4. **Hand off.** Return the policy links and check date, permitted scope, and an
   author-owned checklist of evidence gaps and archive tasks.

For GMD, start with [manuscript types](https://www.geoscientific-model-development.net/about/manuscript_types.html),
[code and data policy](https://www.geoscientific-model-development.net/policies/code_and_data_policy.html),
and the [AI policy](https://www.geoscientific-model-development.net/policies/ai_policy.html).
